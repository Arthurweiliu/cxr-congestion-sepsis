## Interpretation

- **Ten of thirteen** sensitivity analyses support the primary finding; the group C odds ratio ranged from **1.49 to 2.41**.
- Three analyses did not: the 24-h fluid window (S6), complete output recording (S9), and in-hospital mortality (S11).
- The 72-h landmark analysis (S3) is conditional on survival to 72 h and is not directly comparable with the primary estimate.
- S2, S10 and S16 were added during analysis and are labelled as post hoc where they are discussed in the text.
